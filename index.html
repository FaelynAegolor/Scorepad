Here's the deal

A free, offline scorekeeper for board and card games — it does the sums so you don't have to.

  1. 1
    Name your crew

    Tap the placeholder names to swap in real ones. Tap a coloured dot to change a player's colour — everyone gets their own, so there's no more "wait, which line is mine?"

  2. 2
    Rack up the points

    Tap − or + beside a score to nudge, or tap the number itself for a proper keypad. The little pill that pops up above tracks your recent taps, so you can spot a fumble before it's set in stone.

  3. 3
    Prefer a scoresheet?

    Flick on Round-by-round mode from the menu (top right). Enter each player's score on their tile — the tile darkens once it's in — then tap "Add round" to lock the hand in. Flip 7 and Sushi Go! presets are in there too — one tap and the app knows the rules better than half the table.

  4. 4
    Crown the winner

    When the dust settles, tap Finish game for a winner banner, ranked standings, and a shareable image for the group chat. Bragging rights, saved forever.

Everything lives on your device — no accounts, works offline, free forever. Cross our hearts.
