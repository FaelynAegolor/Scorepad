Here's the deal

A free scorekeeper for board and card games — it does the sums so you don't have to.

TL;DRName your players, tap +/− or the keypad to score. Pick a game preset for extra rules, flip on Round-by-round or a category table for those games, expand to Game mode at the table, and hit Finish for a winner screen. Share a code from the menu to sync live with everyone's phones. Tap any step below for the details.
  1. 1Name your crew›

    Tap the placeholder names to swap in real ones. Tap a coloured dot to change a player's colour — everyone gets their own, so there's no more "wait, which line is mine?"

  2. 2Rack up the points›

    Tap − or + beside a score to nudge, or tap the number itself for a proper keypad. The little pill that pops up above tracks your recent taps, so you can spot a fumble before it's set in stone. Pick Star Realms or Yu-Gi-Oh from the menu and the keypad switches to a life total that counts down instead of up.

  3. 3Prefer a scoresheet?›

    Flick on Round-by-round mode from the menu (top right). Enter each player's score on their tile — the tile darkens once it's in — then tap "Add round" to lock the hand in. Flip 7, Sushi Go! (maki and pudding ties split themselves), Star Realms, Yu-Gi-Oh and more presets are in there too — one tap and the app knows the rules better than half the table.

  4. 4Scoring Wingspan or Wyrmspan?›

    Pick either from Game presets and every scoring category shows up on the table straight away — no need to add rounds as you go. Use the little ▲▼ next to a row to move which category your taps and keypad entries fill in next, in whatever order suits how you're tallying the board.

  5. 5Playing at the table?›

    Once everyone's in, tap the expand icon (top right) to flip into Game mode — bigger tiles, no setup clutter, built to read from across the table. Tap it again any time to switch back.

  6. 6Playing across different phones?›

    Open the menu and tap Play together to get a 6-character code. Share it and everyone's taps, renames, and rounds sync live to every phone in the game — no accounts needed, works over the internet too. Leave any time from the same menu and your game quietly goes back to local-only.

  7. 7Crown the winner›

    When the dust settles, tap Finish game for a winner banner, ranked standings, and a shareable image for the group chat. In Star Realms or Yu-Gi-Oh, rankings follow who lasted longest, not who's left with the most life. Bragging rights, saved forever.

Everything lives on your device — no accounts, works offline, free forever. Cross our hearts.
