Welcome to Scorepad.uk

A quick tour

  1. 1
    Add your players

    Type over the default names. Tap a coloured dot to change that player's colour.

  2. 2
    Adjust scores

    Tap the − or + beside a player's score, or tap the number itself to open a keypad. A pill shows recent changes.

  3. 3
    Prefer a table?

    Turn on Round-by-round mode from the top of the menu to log each hand as a row. Presets like Flip 7 and Sushi Go! also live in the menu.

  4. 4
    Finish and share

    Tap Finish game for a winner banner, ranked standings, and a downloadable results image.

Everything saves on this device — no accounts, works offline.
